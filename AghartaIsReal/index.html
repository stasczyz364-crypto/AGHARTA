Agharta is a mysterious underground realm located deep beneath the surface of the Earth. According to legends, it is a vast hidden world inhabited by a highly advanced civilization. It is said that secret passages lead to Agharta, located in remote and difficult-to-reach places around the world. Most of these entrances are believed to be protected and known only to a select few. At the center of Agharta lies Shambhala, an extraordinary city that serves as the capital of this underground world. It is said to contain magnificent buildings, advanced technology, and sources of energy that modern humanity has not yet learned to create. This hidden world would not resemble ordinary caves. Agharta would contain vast plains, lakes, mountains, forests, and enormous open spaces illuminated by a mysterious source of light located within the Earth. Its inhabitants would supposedly live much longer than people on the surface and possess knowledge developed thousands of years before the rise of modern civilizations. Some even claim that they observe the surface world while remaining invisible to its inhabitants.
